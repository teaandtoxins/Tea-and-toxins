Written between shifts

Most days start in a trauma bay
and end with a half-finished cup of tea.

I'm Dr. Shifana, a final-year resident in Emergency Medicine in Tamil Nadu. Tea and Toxins is where the two halves of that sentence collide — the unfiltered version of what actually happens on shift, next to the structured, pattern-based systems I built to survive my own training.

It started as a way to make sense of my own exam prep. It's slowly turned into a place for anyone training in Indian emergency medicine — and for anyone who's ever ended up on the other side of that trauma bay and wanted to understand what was happening to them.
