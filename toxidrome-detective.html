SPECIAL CASE · ONE CLUE CHANGES EVERYTHING 01:20 AM — Emergency Department

"A 19-year-old is brought from a music festival. He is extremely agitated, sweating heavily and repeatedly trying to remove his clothes."

INITIAL READ

Tachycardia + Agitation + Mydriasis

Your gut says: possible sympathomimetic toxicity.

NEW CLUE

Skin is completely dry and bowel sounds are absent.

Now reconsider. Wet skin means sympathomimetic — dry skin flips the whole picture toward anticholinergic toxicity. The mydriasis and tachycardia were never enough on their own.
